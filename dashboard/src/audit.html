What this checks

This audit runs in your browser when you click Run. It reads each page on the site and the data files behind the charts, then reports what it finds. It is deterministic. The same site produces the same result.

What it does not do: it does not use AI, and it does not judge whether the research claims are factually correct. Checking facts needs human or external review. This audit checks that the site is well built and that the data loads, not that the conclusions are true.
